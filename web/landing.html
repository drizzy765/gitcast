how it works:

TRIGGER

02.

CAPTURE

app grabs active window screenshot + git diff. ocr runs locally. nothing leaves your machine yet.

03.

CONTEXT

a minimal terminal prompt asks one question:
> what was the struggle or win? _

type a raw thought. hit enter. done.

04.

GENERATE

groq + deepseek generate 4 post variations in parallel:

task-specific models. under 4 seconds.

05.

PUBLISH

review in the draft room. refine via ai chat. one click to x. done.
