# Insights Dashboard

Track what makes your posts go viral.

## Accessing Insights

Click `[INSIGHTS]` in the left sidebar navigation. Requires at least 5 posts with performance data.

## Metrics collection

### Automatic (X posts published via API)
Tweet ID is stored on publish. Background worker fetches metrics after 7 days via X API. No action required.

### Manual (LinkedIn, clipboard posts)
After 1 day (X) or 7 days (LinkedIn) a reminder banner appears at the top of the dashboard.
Click **REVIEW** and fill in the metrics form.
Takes 30 seconds — go to your post analytics and copy the numbers.

## What Insights shows

### Overview cards
- Total posts published
- Total impressions across all posts
- Average engagement rate
- Current posting streak

### Posting streak grid
GitHub-style contribution grid showing last 30 days. Green squares = posted. Empty = no post.

### Format leaderboard
Which post format gets the most impressions on average.
Ranked table: format, avg impressions, posts, trend.

### Viral coefficient
A score from 0-10 measuring your content's viral potential. Calculated from engagement rate × posting consistency.
Above 7.0 = strong viral potential.

### Optimal posting times
Best day and time window based on your engagement data. Available after 10+ posts with metrics.

### Hashtag performance
Which hashtags add the most impressions on average.

### AI coach message
Rule-based insight at the top of the page:
"Your STRUGGLE posts get 3x more engagement than QUICK_WIN. Post more of those."
